Why olive oil is the star of our kitchen

Olive oil is rich in healthy fats and adds flavour without heavy sauces. We use it to grill, to dress salads and to finish soups.

5 tips for a lighter meal

  1. Start with a salad.
  2. Choose grilled instead of fried.
  3. Share a dessert.
