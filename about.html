About Olive & Ember

Our mission

We believe good food should be simple, honest and shared. Our mission is to serve fresh, tasty meals at a fair price and to make ordering as easy as it can be.

Our objectives

  1. Serve fresh food made from quality ingredients.
  2. Make ordering quick, clear and friendly.
  3. Offer healthy choices for every taste.
  4. Support the local farmers we buy from.

What we offer you

  • Food
    • Daily fresh menu
    • Vegetarian and light options
  • Service
    • Dine in, pick up and delivery
    • Catering for events

Every table gets free filtered H2O. We are also proud to celebrate our 10th year in 2026!
